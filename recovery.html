Recovery

Ten minutes, two or three times a week. After practice, after a game, or on an off day. This is where her body absorbs the training.

The orderStretch first. MAT holds right after, so the muscles wake back up. Then roll out whatever is tight. Pick one tool, not all of them.

2 · Myofascial release about 5 min

Same order as the foam roller video. Big rolls to find the tight spot, then short rolls right on it. One minute per spot is plenty. Knee hurts? The inner quad near the knee is usually the cause.

Watch the tool videos once first. They show the trigger points and how hard to press. After a few times you will not need them.

  • Tight today? Roll it. Fine today? Skip it.
  • Never roll directly on a joint, the knee cap, or the low back bones.
  • Anything that hurts for more than two days is a protocol-and-doctor thing, not a roll-it-out thing.

If something hurts not every week

Only when there is an injury, a sore spot or swelling. Pain that lingers more than two days is a doctor thing, never "play through it".
